Everyday scene 1

People and places

Greetings, introductions, and directions.

  • NamaskaraHello
  • Nimma hesarenu?What is your name?
  • Idu ellide?Where is this?
  • Nera hogiGo straight

Kannada is shown in English letters, so you can say it aloud.

Anand

“Start with people: greetings, introductions, directions. One sentence at a time.”

Rahul

“I just asked for directions, and she answered in Kannada!”

Everyday scene 2

Daily needs

Shopping, prices, food, numbers, and travel.

  • Idu eshtu?How much is this?
  • Ippattu roopaayiTwenty rupees
  • Ondu, eradu, mooruOne, two, three
  • Neeru kodiPlease give me water

Kannada is shown in English letters, so you can say it aloud.

Anand

“Prices and numbers are the heartbeat of the market. Ask, listen, and count along.”

Rahul

“I asked the price and understood the answer. Twenty rupees!”

Everyday scene 3

Work and study

Questions, requests, and everyday interaction.

  • Matte heli, dayavittuPlease say it again
  • Artha aagalillaI did not understand
  • Sahaaya maadteera?Will you help me?
  • KhanditaCertainly

Kannada is shown in English letters, so you can say it aloud.

Anand

“At work or in class, you will need to ask for help and ask people to repeat. Asking is part of learning.”

Rahul

“I can say I didn’t understand, politely, and they happily explain again.”

Health and safety

Basic phrases for comfort, help, and emergencies.

  • Hotte novuStomach ache
  • Chinte bedaDon’t worry
  • Sahaaya maadi!Help!
  • Vaidyaru bekuI need a doctor
